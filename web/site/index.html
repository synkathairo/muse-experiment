Muse experiment demo site

[github repo]

Interactive exhibits. Each runs entirely in your browser.

  • Fluid simulation — drag the boat, stir the water; a real Navier-Stokes solver in WebAssembly
  • Zero-knowledge proof — prove you know x with x³ + x + 5 = 35, revealing nothing. Runs the actual zk-toy Rust prover compiled to WebAssembly.
  • Audio lab — mic, tone, or upload through a hand-rolled FFT, a seven-type biquad filter bank, and YIN pitch detection. All DSP in Rust/WASM; the browser only does I/O.
  • 9×9 Go — play a tiny neural net (130k parameters, trained by self-play) with a live policy heatmap, or scrub the time machine through its training checkpoints.
